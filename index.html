About

I am a Senior Applied Scientist at Bing (Core Search & AI) within Microsoft Copilot.

Before Microsoft I was an Applied Scientist at Amazon Web Services, where I worked on personalized recommendations with AI agents in Amazon Connect. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Rutgers University in 2026, advised by Hao Wang (now at UIUC), and my B.S. from Peking University (Turing Honor Class).

My research covers agentic reinforcement learning and post-training, rewards and evaluation for open-ended tasks, multimodal LLMs, and concept-level interpretability of foundation models.
